Haemoglobin Computational Analysis

Developed independently in my spare time as an exploratory personal research initiative, often collaborating and brainstorming with other friends and peer connections to investigate biomolecular mechanics, structural conformations, and small molecule drug computing interactions.

Repository Part 1

Haemoglobin Structural Dynamics Analysis

This initial module evaluates the sub angstrom conformational shifts occurring during the transition from deoxyhaemoglobin (T state) to oxyhaemoglobin (R state). Using Biopython to parse structural PDB coordinates (1A3N, 1HHO) to structural crystal chains, the script quantifies pocket contraction. By calculating 3D Euclidean distances between the proximal and distal histidine centres across individual subunits, the script quantifies pocket contraction. Beta and alpha chains display distinct conformational responses during the quaternary tetrameric shift, reflecting the molecular principles behind oxygen binding cooperation.

Engineering Focus

  • Parsed PDB coordinate files (1A3N, 1HHO) via Biopython automation scripts.
  • Reconstructed symmetric subunit chains across asymmetrical crystal lattices.
  • Calculated 3D Euclidean distance matrices across proximal and distal histidine residues.
  • Quantified pocket contraction dynamics during T state to R state quaternary transitions.
  • Documented mechanical cooperative linkages governing oxygen binding affinity.
Repository Part 2

Haemoglobin Interaction Analysis and Molecular Documentation

This module investigates the binding mechanism of Voxelotor, an anti-sickling therapeutic agent that stabilises the oxygen bound R state of haemoglobin to prevent polymerisation for drug discovery applications. Custom Python routines target the specific N terminal valine residue on the alpha chain where Voxelotor forms a covalent bond, ensuring docking grids anchor precisely to the biological interaction site rather than relying on broad geometric centroids. PyMOL was utilised in this part to render 3D structural visualisations of these molecular docking poses.

Engineering Focus

  • Targeted N terminal valine residues on haemoglobin alpha chains for precise ligand anchoring.
  • Filtered raw atomic contacts to isolate non-covalent networks and eliminate automated redundancy.
  • Quantified hydrogen bonding, hydrophobic patches, salt bridges, and pi cation interactions.
  • Integrated empirical scoring models to calculate binding free energy (-12.75 kcal/mol).
  • Visualised 3D structural docking poses and binding interactions interactively in PyMOL.
Workspace Media

Repository Part 3

ORCA Computational Workflow & Electronic Structure Analysis

This advanced module implements ab initio density functional theory calculations using ORCA to examine electronic configurations, orbital distributions, and iron centre spin state energetics relevant to therapeutic drug design. By bridging geometric coordinates with quantum chemical calculations, this workflow evaluates electronic charge transfer phenomena associated with ligand binding and structural reconfiguration.

Engineering Focus

  • Executed ab initio density functional theory (DFT) calculations using ORCA.
  • Evaluated iron centre spin state energetics and coordination geometries for drug like candidate complexes.
  • Mapped molecular orbital distributions and electronic charge transfer phenomena.
  • Correlated quantum chemical outputs with biomolecular structural changes.
  • Optimised computational runtime parameters for large coordination complexes.
